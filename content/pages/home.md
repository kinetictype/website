Throughout my career, I've been drawn to clarity. A throughline in all my projects is finding the core message so people understand the essence and actions flow seamlessly. Raised in the New York Metropolitan Area, I moved to Pittsburgh, PA, to study Communication Design at Carnegie Mellon University. 

I was drawn to breaking down complex problems and explaining them clearly, backed by visual sensibilities and technology. I joined the Chicago Tribune as a Visual Journalist and helped move visual explanations online with early interactive graphics. 

From 2009 to 2019, I built user experiences in the San Francisco Bay Area. I returned home to the East Coast in 2019 and focused on large-scale banking. Now in Berlin, I'm teaching Data Storytelling to University Students.
