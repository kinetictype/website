## Goal

The Marketing team owned the Campaign Creation process. When I stepped in with a content designer, we approached the project as a learning opportunity. We learned the process by hearing the team describe it. I then drew a Service Blueprint to illustrate how the teams worked together and how much time they spent in the process.  

> ##### Kiera is a great design partner. She created a framework to help us map the merchant’s journey and identify pain points, gaps, and opportunities. We used the journey to facilitate conversations with our product partners and connect them to the merchant experience. -*S. Colwell,* **JPMorgan Chase Content Designer**

## Outcome

JPMorgan Chase acquired Figg to build a two-sided commerce platform and fill the opportunity gap we illustrated in the Service Design map.
