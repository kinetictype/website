sections:
  - heading: Mapping the TiVo app for iPhone
    text: >+
      TiVo needed the information architecture and spec documentation for its
      app for iPhone. As the information architect, I defined how every screen
      in the app fits together, from connecting to a TiVo box to browsing,
      recording, and managing shows.


      - Setup scans for TiVo boxes, connects to the selected DVR, and signs into
      TiVo.com.

      - Seven main sections: Info, Guide, My Shows, Browse, More, Search, and
      Remote.

  - heading: One map for every screen
    text: >+
      My goal was a single map that the team could use to understand the whole
      app and to specify each screen.


      - Every screen has a number that reflects its place in the hierarchy, from
      0.1 Setup to 4.111 Category List.

      - A key marks web pages, screens whose visibility varies, and sections
      that repeat.

  - heading: Setup, sign-in, and away mode
    text: >+
      The setup flow had to handle more than the happy path. The map shows what
      happens when a TiVo box is found, when it is missing, when sign-in fails,
      and when someone continues as a guest.


      - Paths for checking again, sign-in help on TiVo.com, and in-app help and
      troubleshooting

      - An away mode that connects to the Guide and Browse sections

      - An open question for the team: what is the guest experience?

  - heading: The application map
    text: The main sections branch into the screens beneath them. My Shows sorts
      recordings by date, A–Z, episode, and folder, with states for each
      recording such as delete in 3 days, delete in 24 hours, and kept until
      deleted. Browse leads to collections, movies, TV, sports, and categories.
