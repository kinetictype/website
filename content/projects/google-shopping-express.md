In 2013, Google announced the transition from the old **Google Shopping to** **Google Express**, an evolved version of the platform that lets users buy products from different vendor websites and marketplaces directly on Google Express. Our team consulted with Google to propose a solution that would handle everything on the platform, from collecting payments to delivering products. At the time, it was mostly used for grocery shopping.
