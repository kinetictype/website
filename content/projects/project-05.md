Goal

Design a vision for [help.fitbit.com](http://help.fitbit.com) as a direct self-service platform that reduces incoming help calls. People were confused by the existing site.

Timeline

Not stated in the source files (heat map research dated July 14, 2016).

Outcome

- Service blueprint and ecosystem/system map
- Heat map and metrics analysis
- Three personas from quantitative data (Josh, Allie, Susan) and three search scenarios
- 200 articles regrouped from 17 categories into 4
-  Search wizard, article section navigation, "Recent Issues" banner, chatbot
- Responsive, mobile-first designs

What Positive Outcomes Emerged

- Bridged customer support and product design (separate teams, separate buildings); started regular work with the onboarding team
- The project was put on hold due to competing priorities and declining revenue; the site never moved to the new platform

My Role  
Lead and sole designer: research synthesis, framework and vision.

My Team  
"Design team of one."   
Worked directly with three stakeholders and a program manager.
