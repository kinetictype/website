  - heading: How might we create a self-service platform?
    text: >+
      Fitbit wanted to improve its customer support website as part of an
      internal initiative to redefine the self-service experience. The support
      site drew about 1.5 million visitors a month, most of them searching for
      an answer to a specific issue. Most visitors who filed a support case had
      never tried self-service. Our hypothesis was that if people could find
      help on the website, fewer of them would need to call Fitbit. We had to
      rethink the entire site's navigation.

  - heading: Designing mobile first
    text: Metrics showed that most people accessed help from their phones while on

      experience as well. The new navigation starts with the customer's issue,

      and the community forum always close at hand.

---
Design a vision for [help.fitbit.com](http://help.fitbit.com) as a direct self-service platform that reduces incoming help calls. People were confused by the existing site. Bridged customer support and product design (separate teams, separate buildings); started regular work with the onboarding team. The project was put on hold due to competing priorities and declining revenue; the site never moved to the new platform.  
Lead and sole designer: research synthesis, framework, and vision.
