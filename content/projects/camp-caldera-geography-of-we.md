sections:
  - heading: Mapping story, memory, and place
    text: Caldera's programs reach artists across Oregon, from Portland to Central
      Oregon. Caldera Arts wanted to add a section to its website that showcases
      the work of the young artists who attend its programs. The Geography of We
      would show each project together with where the artist comes from, the
      type of work, and the year it was made. The collection held 90 to 100
      projects in image, writing, sound, and video. The design team thought a
      map would let visitors discover the work through the places where it came
      from.

  - heading: Design a Map to Carry the Brand
    text: My goal was to create a custom geographic experience that felt like
      Caldera while also letting people find a project quickly. How might we let
      visitors explore the work by place, media, and year? Visitors can filter
      by place, media, and year, or search by an artist's name or school.

  - heading: Sketching the map

      thumbnails on the same screen, and designed map pins that show each
      project's media type at a glance. The sketches also raised an early
      question for the content model, what happens when a project has no
      location?
