  - heading: A mark for a modular space
    text: >+
      Merchandise Mart Properties runs the Merchandise Mart in Chicago, a vast
      building of showrooms and exhibition space that is constantly rearranged.
      At 3st, the studio of Rick Valicenti, we developed a logo concept that
      would express a modular space. Blocks represent the letters of the company
      name, so each word becomes a set of blocks of a matching length.

      Merchandise Mart changes, while staying recognizable. How might we build

      could be arranged in different positions and combinations to imply the
      meaning of the Merchandise Mart through the form.

Working with [Rick Valicenti](https://www.designboom.com/design/rick-vallecenti-3st-interview/) was a career highlight. I designed the logo concept, color palette, block-theme variations, and an animated version of the mark for Merchandise Mart Properties. In the end, they wanted something less dynamic and more understated. In the meantime, we explored movable identity marks together, which was a lot of fun!
