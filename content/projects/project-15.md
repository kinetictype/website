Before our mobile phones had video, there was a booming company called Flip. These tiny devices were the size of a cigarette box and could plug into your laptop to download photos for sharing. Flipshare was the software application that let you share and favorite your videos. I joined the team to design the Android app that would sync with the desktop app.
